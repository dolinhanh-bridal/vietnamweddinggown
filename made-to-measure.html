Before you order

Good to know

  • Order at least 4 months before your wedding, to leave time for shipping and anything unexpected
  • You pay first, then we start the next step
  • Payment goes to our bank account in Vietnam, by Wise, SWIFT bank transfer or Remitly (how to pay)
  • Before production, we both confirm in writing: the design, fabric, measurements and beadwork
  • Cancelled orders are not refunded
  • Every gown is handmade and measured online, so small variations are normal; it may need alterations where you receive it, at your cost
  • The silhouette comes out about 80–90% like your photos and the beadwork about 50–60%. This is how close the design will be, not a promise of fit
  • The price depends on the design, fabric and beadwork, and stays fixed once quoted
  • Planning to lose or gain weight? Tell us before you measure
